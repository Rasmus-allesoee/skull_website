## Overview

The raccoon dog is a canid in the order Carnivora. The accepted name and hierarchy used for this exhibit agree with the reviewed GBIF match and the Mammal Diversity Database record. [cite:gbif-backbone] [cite:mammal-diversity-database]

## Skull identification
